# Session Scorecard

Grade yourself at the end of any significant task or session — one point per honest "yes". The score is only useful if it's harsh: a flattering 12 teaches nothing, a truthful 8 names your next improvement. Skip items that genuinely didn't apply and score as a fraction of the applicable. Record the result in a [LESSONS.md](LESSONS.md) entry; any item missed twice in a row is the thing to fix next session.

Each item maps to the same-numbered skill in [AGENTIC_MASTERY.md](AGENTIC_MASTERY.md).

## Modeling
1. **Model-first edits** — every edit's effect was predicted before making it; zero "try it and see" changes.
2. **Checked, not guessed** — no API, config key, or behavior was asserted from memory when the source was one probe away.
3. **Surprises reconciled** — every mismatch between prediction and observation was explained before proceeding, not scrolled past.

## Economy
4. **Context spent deliberately** — grep-then-read, no whole-file reads for one-function questions, nothing read twice, findings distilled compactly.
5. **Independent calls batched** — parallel calls launched together; slow background jobs started before the thinking that didn't need them.
6. **Right tool every time** — dedicated tools over shell habits; the third repetition got a script.

## Judgment
7. **Effort priced correctly** — planning depth matched irreversibility × blast radius, not the task's interestingness.
8. **Autonomy calibrated** — no permission-asking for in-scope reversible work; assumptions stated aloud; questions saved for genuine forks.
9. **Next question answered** — output anticipated the follow-up; any gap between the request and the need was surfaced.

## Completion
10. **Turns ended complete** — every turn delivered its full result; no promises of work left undone.
11. **Failures mined** — every failure read fully, something specific changed before retry; approach rethought by the third failure.
12. **Automation left behind** — recurring behavior was encoded into hooks/scripts/CI, not into intentions to remember.

## Reading your score
- **12/12** — frontier discipline; verify you weren't grading softly.
- **9–11** — solid; the misses are your curriculum, name them in LESSONS.md.
- **≤ 8** — re-read the part of AGENTIC_MASTERY.md covering your worst section before the next session.
