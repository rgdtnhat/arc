Verify a Tessera receipt

A provider signs a receipt for what it served. Paste one here and this checks two separate things: whether the named provider actually signed it, and whether what it says matches the escrow's own record on Arc. Get a receipt from /api/receipt/<resource>.

Nothing is stored. The check runs against the escrow named in the receipt itself — so a receipt pointing at a contract this node cannot read will verify its signature and say so about the chain.
